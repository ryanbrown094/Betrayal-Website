Refunds and cancellations

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Changed your mind?

If you haven’t used your booking code yet, you can cancel within 14 days of buying it and we’ll refund you in full. Send us a message through our contact form with your booking code or the email address you paid with. Or use this model form: “I cancel my contract for a Table booking for The Betrayal (your code), bought on (date). Name, email, date.” We’ll cancel the booking and refund you within 14 days. The refund goes to the card you paid with.

Once you’ve opened a room with your booking code, the 14-day right to cancel no longer applies. At checkout you agreed that the booking code could be supplied straight away, and confirmed you understood you would lose the right to cancel once you started using it. This is allowed under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013.

Subscriptions

Cancel any time with “Manage subscription” on the home page, using your code. There’s no charge for cancelling: you won’t be billed again, and your code keeps working until the end of the month you’ve paid for. We don’t refund part-months, except where the law requires it or something went wrong on our side. If you haven’t used your code to open a room yet, you can cancel within 14 days of subscribing and get the first month back in full.

Something went wrong?

If the game didn’t work properly, whether or not you’d started, tell us what happened through our contact form. Please write within 30 days of your party if you can. Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 we will:

  • try to fix the problem, or give you a new booking code, within a reasonable time; and
  • if we can’t, give you some or all of your money back, depending on how much of the party it affected.

We’re a small team and want every party to go well. If an outage on our side spoiled your evening, we’ll refund you.

Party postponed?

A room only starts its 48 hours when you first enter the booking code, so you can keep an unused booking for a later date. Unused booking codes don’t expire.

How refunds are paid

We refund the way you paid, through Stripe. Refunds usually reach your account within 5–10 working days of being issued, depending on your bank. We don’t charge a fee. If you used a discount, such as the broken seal, we refund what you actually paid.

These terms don’t affect your legal rights. See the full terms of sale.
