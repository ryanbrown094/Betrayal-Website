Changed your mind?

If you haven’t used your pass yet, you can cancel within 14 days of buying it and we’ll refund you in full. Email [contact email] with your pass code or the email address you paid with. Or use this model form: “I cancel my contract for the Cloak & Candle party pass [code], bought on [date]. Name, email, date.” We’ll cancel the pass and refund you within 14 days. The refund goes to the card you paid with.

Once you’ve opened a room with your pass, the 14-day right to cancel no longer applies. At checkout you agreed that the pass could be supplied straight away, and confirmed you understood you would lose the right to cancel once you started using it. This is allowed under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013.

Something went wrong?

If the game didn’t work properly, whether or not you’d started, tell us what happened at [contact email]. Please write within [30] days of your party if you can. Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 we will:

  • try to fix the problem, or give you a new pass, within a reasonable time; and
  • if we can’t, give you some or all of your money back, depending on how much of the party it affected.

We’re a small team and want every party to go well. If an outage on our side spoiled your evening, we’ll refund you.
