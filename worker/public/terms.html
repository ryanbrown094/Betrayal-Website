2. What you’re buying

When you book a Table (£10, one party), you get a booking code, which looks like OATH-XXXX-XXXX. It opens one online game room, your Table, for one party. It is digital content: nothing is posted to you.

  • When you first enter the booking code, it opens your Table. The Table can be played for 48 hours from that moment.
  • After 48 hours the Table becomes read-only. It is deleted, with everything in it, 7 days after that.
  • One booking code is for one party of 8 to 24 players in one place. You may not resell a booking code, or use one for more than one event.
  • Entering the booking code again within the 48 hours brings back the same Table.

A Regular subscription (£20 a month) gives you one code that opens a new Table whenever you like, for as long as the subscription is paid. Each Table works like one bought on its own (open 48 hours, then read-only, then deleted), and you can have one game open at a time: starting a new game closes the one that was open. The code is for you and your own parties; you may not share it with other hosts.

To play, you need an internet connection and a phone for each player; a TV is optional. If you choose to play Classic, you need a TV or large screen with a laptop or other device that can show a web page on it, a phone for the Keeper, and a blindfold and a small chalkboard (or whiteboard) for each player. We don’t supply these.
