A Invitation is a code (it looks like CNDL-XXXX-XXXX) that opens one online game room for one party. It is digital content: nothing is posted to you.

  • When you first enter the pass, it opens a room. The room can be played for 48 hours from that moment.

An Unlimited subscription (£20 a month) gives you one code that opens a new game room whenever you like, for as long as the subscription is paid. Each room works like an Invitation room (48 hours, then read-only, then deleted), and you can have one game open at a time: starting a new game closes the one that was open. The code is for you and your own parties; you may not share it with other hosts.

To play, you need a TV or large screen with a laptop or other device that can show a web page on it, a phone for the host, and an internet connection. You also need the props described on the home page, such as blindfolds and chalkboards. We don’t supply these.
