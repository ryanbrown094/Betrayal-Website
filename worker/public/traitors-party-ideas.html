Traitors party ideas

A traitors-themed party is a dinner party with a secret: a few of your guests are traitors, and by the end of the night everyone else has to work out who. Here’s how to throw one for 8 to 24 friends, from the invitations to the final reveal.

The plan in one minute

  • Guests: 8 to 24 adults. 8 to 16 is the sweet spot.
  • Dress code: dark and dramatic: cloaks, capes, tweed, black tie or anything a castle would approve of.
  • The room: one long table, low light and candles.
  • Food: a banquet you can share, then the game after dinner.
  • The game: secret roles, a vote each round and a victim each night. Run it by hand, or let everyone’s phones run it for you.
  • The prize: a pot the loyal players win if they catch every traitor.

Invitations

Tell guests the theme and the dress code, and that there will be a game with secret roles, but nothing more. The suspense starts on the doorstep. Something like:

You are summoned to a night of loyalty and lies. Dress for a castle. Some of you will be traitors. Trust nobody.

Ask everyone to come with a charged phone if you’ll play on phones, and give a firm start time: the game works best when everyone is there for the first vote.

Dress code

Dark colours, capes and cloaks, tweed and tartan, long coats, gloves and the odd dramatic hat. A few spare cloaks or scarves by the door mean nobody feels left out. Some hosts give the eventual winners a sash or a badge to wear for the rest of the night.

Setting the room

  • One long table where everyone can see each other’s faces. Accusations land better across a table.
  • Low lights, candles (battery ones if there are drinks about) and a dark tablecloth.
  • A TV for the scores and the reveals, if you have one. It turns the game into a show for the room.
  • Somewhere for the traitors to meet unseen, if you’re playing by hand. On phones they don’t need it: their phones tell them who their fellow traitors are.

Food and drink

Go for a banquet: sharing boards, pies, a big stew or a roast, and bread to tear. Keep it easy to eat while talking, because there will be a lot of talking. A dark “poisoned” cocktail is a good welcome drink (blackberry and gin, or a blackcurrant mocktail), and something warm works in winter. Plan the game for after the main course, or play between courses with the plates cleared for each vote.

The game

About one guest in four or five is secretly a traitor: 2 for 8 to 10 players, 3 for 11 to 15, 4 for 16 to 22 and 5 for 23 or 24. The traitors know each other. Each round there’s a challenge, then everyone debates and votes someone out, then at night the traitors choose a victim. The loyal players win if they vote out every traitor; the traitors win if even one survives to the end.

You can run it by hand with role cards and a host who doesn’t play: our guide to hosting a traitors-style game at home has the rules, the numbers and a timetable. By hand, plan on about 3 to 3½ hours for 8 to 12 players.

Prizes

Put up a prize pot. Chocolate coins are the classic: the loyal players win the pot if they catch every traitor, and if a traitor survives, the traitors share it instead. Small awards at the end go down well too (the best liar, the worst accuser, the most suspicious face), and The Betrayal hands out up to six of its own.

A Christmas traitors party

The theme works brilliantly for Christmas: it’s a game everyone plays at once, so nobody is left watching from the sofa. Swap the cloaks for festive jumpers if you like, serve mulled wine as the “poison” and play after Christmas dinner. For an office party, play on phones: it works for teams of 8 to 24, needs no setting up beyond a TV if you have one, and colleagues who’ve never met learn a lot about each other in an hour.

Questions

How many people do you need for a traitors party?

At least 8, and up to 24. The game is at its best with 8 to 16, when everyone gets a say in every vote.

How long does the game take?

Played on phones with The Betrayal, about an hour for 8 to 10 players, about an hour and a half for 12 to 16, and about two hours for 20 to 24. Run by hand, allow 3 hours or more.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. With The Betrayal everyone plays in their phone’s browser, with no app to install and no account to make.
