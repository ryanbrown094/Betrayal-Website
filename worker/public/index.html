questions

Before you ask

Does everyone need a phone?

No. Only the host uses one. Guests watch the TV, wear blindfolds when told, and chalk names at the Reckoning. At a few moments the host hands the phone round for a secret vote.

What does a pass get me?

One room for one party (£15). It stays open for 48 hours from when you first open it, so you can set up in the afternoon and play into the small hours.

What about the monthly subscription?

£20 a month gets you one code that starts a new game whenever you like — one game at a time, each open for 48 hours. It renews monthly until you cancel, which you can do any time with “Manage subscription”.

Does the host get to play?

The host runs the game — like a presenter. They know who the Cloaks are from the start, so they don’t play a role.

Why “Cloak & Candle”?

Every guest has a candle burning on the TV. The Cloaks hide under the cloak. Each night a candle goes out — and the Candles have until the last one to pull the cloak away.

Murder or poison?

Your choice, at setup. Murdered players are tapped in the night and leave the table while everyone is blindfolded. Poisoned players find out in the night, announce it at dawn, say their last words, then leave.

What if the Wi-Fi drops?

Both screens reconnect by themselves, and the phone keeps every change until the server has it. Nothing is lost.
