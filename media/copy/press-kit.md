## Long description (about 150 words)
The Betrayal is a dinner-party game of loyalty and lies. It opens with the Oath: every guest, blindfolded, swears loyalty to the table, and as they do, the Keeper walks behind the chairs. A tap on the shoulder makes a few of them Forsworn. Trials fill a shared Purse and win Sanctuary from the night. At the Reckoning every guest chalks the name of the person they suspect, and each night a candle goes out. Dinner can be served in three courses, between the rounds.

The TV (any laptop cast to it) carries the show: title cards, a candle for every guest, a full orchestral score, and a narrator if you want one. The Keeper’s phone runs everything else: five questions to set up, then every line to read, every vote counted, every secret kept. The Loyal win if they exile every Forsworn, and the Purse decides how good the prize is. If a single Forsworn survives, the night is theirs.

## Feature bullets
- Five questions, then play: who’s playing, how long, murder or poison, the prize, who’s keeping. Everything else is pre-set, and can be changed under Customise.
- The host can play too: choose “A friend will keep, and I’ll play”, add yourself to the players and hand the phone to a friend, who presses Start. You never see the roles.
- An easy first night: your first game is Classic, and the twists wait for your second.
- A proper show on the TV: the Oath, title cards, a candlelit Reckoning, night scenes, the Unmasking and a full adaptive score. Switch on the narrator and the TV reads the Keeper’s lines aloud.
- The Keeper never has to improvise: every line to read is on the phone, and nothing said aloud gives a secret away.
- Served in three courses: the game can stop for a starter, the main course and dessert, each in its place between the rounds.
- The Purse decides the prize: fill half of it for a better prize, all of it for the best. The prizes stay secret until the end.
- The dead keep playing: at each Reckoning the Ghosts chalk who they think will go, and the TV names the ones who called it.
- It ends with awards: up to six, from The Grand Deceiver to Last Oath Standing.
