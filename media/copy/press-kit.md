## Short description (about 50 words)
Cloak & Candle is a hosted party game for 8 to 24 people. A few guests are secretly Cloaks; everyone else must find them before it's too late. Your TV shows the missions, the Reckoning and the candlelit nights. Your phone gives the host every line to read. No downloads, no accounts.

## Long description (about 150 words)
Cloak & Candle is a dinner-party game of loyalty and lies. Every guest gets a candle on the TV. When the titles roll, the host walks the table while everyone sits blindfolded, and a tap on the shoulder hands someone the cloak: they are a Cloak. From then on the evening belongs to the game: missions that fill a shared pot, secret shields and blades won in hidden rounds, a Reckoning where every guest chalks the name of the person they suspect — and, each night, a candle goes out.

The TV — any laptop cast to it — carries the show: title cards, a candle for every guest, a full orchestral score. The host's phone runs everything else: it plans the evening before anyone arrives, deals the missions, counts the votes, keeps the secrets, and gives the host every line to read out. The Candles win the pot if they exile every Cloak. If a single Cloak survives, the prize is theirs.

## Feature bullets
- Plan once, then play: every decision is made on the phone before guests arrive; the game runs the evening from there.
- A proper show on the TV: title cards, candlelit Reckoning, night scenes, reveals and a full adaptive score.
- The host never has to improvise: every line to read is on the phone, and nothing said aloud gives a secret away.
- Secret prizes with real stakes: hidden shields, blades that double a vote, the Champion, and the Witness who meets a Cloak face to face.
- The dead keep playing: Ghosts judge from beyond the table and can still take the prize.
- Ends with awards — The Grand Deceiver, The Sharpest Eye, The Lightning Rod and more.
