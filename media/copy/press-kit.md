## Long description (about 150 words)
The Betrayal is a dinner-party game of loyalty and lies. It opens with the Sacrament: every guest, blindfolded, swears an oath to the table, and as they do, the Keeper walks behind the chairs. A tap on the shoulder makes someone Forsworn. From then on the evening belongs to the game. Trials fill a shared Purse. Sanctuary is won in secret rounds, and a Casting Vote counts twice. At the Reckoning every guest chalks the name of the person they suspect, and each night a candle goes out.

The TV (any laptop cast to it) carries the show: title cards, a candle for every guest, a full orchestral score. The Keeper's phone runs everything else. It plans the evening before anyone arrives, deals the Trials, counts the votes, keeps the secrets and gives the Keeper every line to read. The Loyal win the Purse if they exile every Forsworn. If a single Forsworn survives, the night is theirs.

## Feature bullets
- Plan once, then play: a step-by-step setup explains every choice and recommends one. The game runs the evening from there.
- A proper show on the TV: the Sacrament, title cards, a candlelit Reckoning, night scenes, the Unmasking and a full adaptive score.
- The Keeper never has to improvise: every line to read is on the phone, and nothing said aloud gives a secret away.
- Secret prizes with real stakes: hidden Sanctuary, a Casting Vote, the Crown, and one special prize near the end that nobody sees coming.
- The dead keep playing: Ghosts judge from beyond the table and can still take the prize.
- It ends with awards: The Grand Deceiver, The Sharpest Eye, Last Oath Standing and more.
