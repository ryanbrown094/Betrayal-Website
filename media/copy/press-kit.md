## Long description (about 150 words)
The Betrayal is a dinner-party game of loyalty and lies, and everyone plays on their own phone. Guests join like a pub quiz: scan the QR code on the TV, or go to thebetrayal.co.uk/join and type the Table code and their name. Then the Oath arrives on every phone, a question nobody else can see: would you like to be Forsworn? A few are. They see each other on their phones; the Loyal have one evening to find them.

Fifteen Trials, played on phones (quizzes, word games, charades where only the actor sees the word), fill a shared Purse and win Sanctuary from the night. At the Reckoning everyone votes on their phone. At night the Forsworn choose, while everyone else taps too so nobody can tell who’s choosing; the victim finds out in secret. The host plays as well: the game plans itself. Prefer it the old-fashioned way? Classic has a Keeper, blindfolds and chalk.

## Feature bullets
- A phone each, nothing to buy: no blindfolds, no chalkboards, no app, no accounts. Guests join like a pub quiz.
- The host plays too: their phone just moves the evening on. The game plans its own Trials, courses and Reckonings, and never shows the host a role.
- Secrets stay secret: roles, votes, night choices and murders arrive privately on each phone. The Forsworn see each other; nobody else sees anything.
- Nobody can tell who’s choosing: at night everyone taps their phone, and only the Forsworn’s choices count.
- Fifteen Trials on your phone: type quiz answers, guess numbers, make words, play rock-paper-scissors, and act out charades with the word sent only to the actor.
- The Purse decides the prize: the Loyal build it; the Forsworn, if they win, take what it fell short, so they sabotage quietly.
- Served in three courses: the game can stop for a starter, the main course and dessert, each in its place between the rounds.
- The TV is optional, and worth it: the QR code, the questions, the votes and the reveals, with music and a narrator if you want one.
- The dead keep playing: Ghosts still call each Reckoning from beyond the table.
- It ends with awards: up to six, from The Grand Deceiver to The Ghost Who Saw.
- Prefer it the old-fashioned way? Classic runs from one phone, with a Keeper, blindfolds and chalk.
