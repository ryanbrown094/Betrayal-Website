# Setting up a game: Modern first, then Classic

Every Table can be played two ways, and the host chooses when they open it. **Modern** is the headline: everyone plays on their own phone, and the host plays too. **Classic** is for anyone who’d prefer it the old-fashioned way: one phone, a Keeper who doesn’t play, blindfolds and chalkboards. Nothing is dealt until someone presses **Start**.

## Modern: six steps

1. **Book a Table** (£10, one party) or use a Regular’s booking code.
2. **Open your Table:** enter the booking code at thebetrayal.co.uk, on the phone you’ll play on. It opens one Table for one party of 8 to 24, open for 48 hours from first use.
3. **Choose Modern.** (“Your Table is open. How will you play?”)
4. **Type your name** and tap **Open the Table**. You’re the host, and you play too: your phone runs the evening (you begin each Trial and call the breaks and the Reckoning), everything else runs itself, and you never see anyone’s role.
5. **Share the code.** Guests go to **thebetrayal.co.uk/join** and type the six-character **Table code** and their name, or scan the **QR code** on the TV (open `thebetrayal.co.uk/t/CODE` on a laptop and cast it; the TV is optional). The lobby fills with names as they join.
6. **Press Start** when everyone’s in (“Start with 12 players”). Then the Oath arrives on every phone.

### The host’s choices (in the lobby, while guests join)

| choice | options | pre-set / notes |
|---|---|---|
| How do the Forsworn kill? | Poison / Murder | poison. Poison: the victim finds out on their phone and gets last words at dawn. Murder: the victim finds out on their phone and leaves the table at dawn |
| Sanctuary | Secret / Public | secret. Secret: only the winner knows they hold it (a Forsworn winner gets nothing, and learns so). Public: everyone sees who won it |
| The prize | any words | the default is a box of chocolates; hosts set their own |
| Better prizes for a fuller Purse | optional: one at a half-full Purse, one at a full Purse | if the Forsworn win it works the other way: half empty wins them the better prize, empty the best. Nobody sees these until the end |
| Twists | On / Off | on. On: once each, the Casting Vote, the Crown, and the Confessor in the final. Off: Sanctuary and the Temptation only |
| Dinner in courses | On / Off | off. For a Table that’s eating together: On serves a starter, a main and dessert between the rounds. Off: no set courses, and the host can still call a short break |

The Purse target is 2,000 points. Everything else the game plans for itself: how many Forsworn (2 for 8–10 players, 3 for 11–15, 4 for 16–22, 5 for 23–24, dealt with the Oath answers in mind), how many rounds, which Trials and when, how many leave each round so the final starts with five, and, if dinner in courses is on, when each course comes.

### During the game

The host’s phone shows a small host card: **Begin** the next Trial, or **a short break first** (10 minutes, with **+5 minutes** and **End the break**), then the Reckoning, **Reveal the night** and **On to the next round**. **Pause** stops every clock. **Tools** handles the awkward moments: let a phone that lost its place back in, take someone who is ill out of the game (nobody learns their role), hand the host’s controls to someone else, play the music on this phone if there’s no TV, or move on when someone is stuck. At the end, **Play again with the same people**.

## Classic: the old-fashioned way

Enter the booking code, choose **Classic**, and the phone becomes the Keeper’s. The Keeper sets the game up before guests arrive: **five questions and a PIN**, then a summary that’s ready to start. Every other choice is pre-set, worked out from those answers, and can be changed under **Customise**. Nothing reaches the TV until someone presses **Start**. You’ll need a TV and a laptop to cast to it, and a blindfold, a small chalkboard and chalk for each guest. Screenshots: `04-screenshots/classic/phone/phone-0*-setup-*.png`.

### The five questions

| # | question | choices | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who’s playing? | 8 to 24 names, in seating order | seat order is the TV’s candle order and the order boards are turned. Best with 8 to 16 |
| 2 | How long? | the Short game, or a full evening | the Short game takes about 2 hours for 8 to 10 players: one Trial a round, no Interval, no dinner break. A full evening is about 3 to 3½ hours for 8 to 12 players, longer for bigger tables (about 5 hours for 16, and 6 or more for 20 and up), and dinner adds half an hour to an hour if you choose it under Customise; the phone estimates it for the table |
| 3 | Murder or poison? | poison / murder | how the Forsworn kill. Poison: the victim is told in the night and announces it at dawn. Murder: the victim is led away under blindfold |
| 4 | The prize | up to three prizes | the prize; a better one if the Purse reaches half its target; the best if it’s full. They stay secret until the end |
| 5 | Who’s keeping? | the host keeps / “A friend will keep, and I’ll play” | with the second, the host includes themselves in the players and names the Keeper. The host hands the phone to the friend, who presses Start, so the host never sees the roles |
| — | Keeper PIN | 4 to 8 digits | opens the Keeper screen on another phone if this one dies. A friend who keeps can change it when the phone is handed over |

Then **Ready to start**: a summary of the game, with every pre-set choice listed and **Customise** to change any of them.

### The first game, and the second

- **The first game** played on this phone: Sanctuary won openly, Ghosts, an Interval. The twists are off: the Casting Vote, the Crown, the Temptation, the Confessor, the Whisper, the Last Offer and Errands.
- **Your second night:** once a game has been played on the phone, setup recommends the twists.

### Under Customise

Each choice is pre-set; this is what can be changed and how it relates to the rest.

| choice | options | pre-set / notes |
|---|---|---|
| How many Forsworn? | recommended ±1 | 2 for 8–10, 3 for 11–15, 4 for 16–22, 5 for 23–24 |
| How many rounds? | the range the table allows (e.g. 5 for 12, 8–11 for 24), each with who’s left after each round and an estimated length | the count that best fits the length chosen |
| Trials each round | by the clock / one / two | one in the Short game |
| Dinner | none, one break, or three courses | none. For a Table that’s eating together: one 25-minute dinner break after the round you choose, or three courses, which need four or more rounds: a starter after round one (15 minutes), the main course mid-game (25), dessert before the final (15). None in the Short game |
| An Interval before each vote? | yes / no | yes, except in the Short game |
| Should the TV read your lines? | the Keeper reads / the TV reads | the Keeper reads. With the TV reading, each line gets a ▶ TV button, and the Menu switches it on or off during the game |
| Do the dead stay in the game? | Ghosts / out for good | Ghosts. Turning Ghosts off switches off the Statue and the Ghosts’ call |
| Which Trials can your room play? | any of 15 | all; presets: all, or only games at the table |
| Sanctuaries | mixed / always public / hidden where possible / none | always public in a first game; none also removes the Crown and the Temptation |
| The Casting Vote | once / none | off in a first game; breaks a tie at one Reckoning. Shows the round it’ll appear in |
| The Crown | once / none | off in a first game; needs Sanctuaries. Shows its round |
| The Temptation | once / none | off in a first game; needs Sanctuaries. PURSE or SELF |
| The Confessor (the Keeper is told what it is) | on / off | off in a first game |
| Whisper | once / never | off in a first game |
| The Last Offer | once / never | off in a first game; wording changes with the Whisper on or off |
| Forsworn Errands | on / off | off in a first game; only with an Interval |
| The Purse | unit (pts, £…) and target | 2,000 pts; the target also sets the prize thresholds (half, and full) |
| Who pays for wrong answers? | Forsworn only / everyone (Forsworn double) | Forsworn only |
| A name for your night | the TV title | “The Betrayal”; e.g. “Sophie’s 40th”. Also links to the question bank switches |

Estimated lengths for every table size, and the rounds the Casting Vote and the Crown appear in, are in `table-sizes.csv`.
