# The Betrayal: words, names and naming

Locked in 28 September 2026, updated 29 September 2026 (the Table, the Oath, the Casting Vote, PURSE or SELF, and two tiers of words). This replaces the Cloak & Candle vocabulary. The **The Betrayal** column of the rename table is what the game, the site and every piece of marketing move to.

## The name

**The Betrayal.** Always with “The”, and both words capitalised: “The Betrayal”, never “Betrayal” alone. Where a space can’t go (URLs, file names, handles), write `thebetrayal`. The site is **thebetrayal.co.uk**. Never pair the name with another game’s or show’s name.

## The idea in one line

Everyone at the table swears an oath. A few of them are lying. They are the **Forsworn**, and the **Loyal** have one evening to find them.

## Two tiers of words

### Public words

Public copy (ads, the press kit, video scripts, outreach, the content calendar, store listings, the public parts of the marketing pack) uses only these:

**the Forsworn · the Loyal · the Keeper · the Oath · Trial · the Reckoning · Sanctuary · the Purse · Ghost · the Unmasking**

Plus plain words (exile, night, dawn, dinner, the Interval, oath, candle, blindfold, chalkboard) and the product words (a Table, Book a Table, booking code, a Regular, Become a Regular, Table code, the Short game).

### In-game words

**the Casting Vote · the Crown · the Temptation · the Whisper · the Last Offer · Errands · the Confessor · Trust or Doubt · Honour or Betray**

These belong to the game. The TV and the Keeper’s phone explain each one when it happens, and the internal game guide explains them all. In public copy, never name them: call them **twists the TV explains when they happen**. The Confessor is never named or described in public, not even as a hint.

## Capitals

Capitalised: **The Betrayal**, **the Forsworn**, **the Loyal**, **Ghost**, **the Keeper**, **the Oath** (the opening scene), **Trial**, **the Reckoning**, **the Interval**, **Sanctuary**, **the Purse**, **the Unmasking**; in-game, **the Casting Vote**, **the Crown**, **the Temptation**, **the Whisper**, **the Last Offer**, **Errand**, **the Confessor** (internal), **Trust or Doubt**, **Honour or Betray**; product, **Table**, **Table code**, **Regular**, **the Short game**, **Classic**, **Customise**.

Lower case: exile, night, dawn, poison, murder, oath (the promise itself: “everyone swore an oath”), seal, candle, blindfold, chalkboard, dinner, starter, main course, dessert, booking code.

What people chalk is written in capitals: PURSE, SELF, TRUST, DOUBT.

## Length: say it honestly

- **Best at 8 to 16 players.** The game takes 8 to 24.
- **8 to 12 players:** about 3 to 4 hours with dinner.
- **The Short game:** about 2 hours for 8 to 10 players (one Trial a round, no Interval, no dinner break).
- **Bigger tables run longer:** 20 or more, about 6 hours.
- State the capacity as a fact; never sell it as a benefit. No “up to 24 players, one very long night”.

## Marks and type

The brand guidelines have the detail; in brief:

- **The primary lockup** (the hooded figure and the wordmark) is unchanged. It signs everything where the name needs to be said.
- **The secondary mark** is the cracked wax seal, *the broken oath*: `the-betrayal-seal.svg` / `.png` in full colour (wine wax, a candle embossed, a rose crack) and `the-betrayal-seal-mono-rose.svg`, `-ivory.svg` and `-ink.svg` in one colour. Use it for the favicon and app icon, social avatars, stickers and packaging seals. It never replaces the lockup.
- **Type:** Grenze Gotisch (blackletter) only for the wordmark and the big TV moments. Headings in IM Fell DW Pica. Body and UI in Alegreya Sans; eyebrows in Alegreya Sans SC.

## Voice

Candlelit, dry, a little theatrical, never gory. Short sentences. The Keeper speaks to the table like someone who knows more than they’re saying: “Everyone here swore an oath tonight. Not everyone meant it.” Use British spelling and British words (Interval, not intermission; queue, not line). The calls to action are **Book a Table** and **Become a Regular**.

## The Oath

The Oath is the opening scene and the brand’s most theatrical moment. Keep it theatrical, never religious: no crosses, altars, priests or holy books. Show it with hands flat on the table, blindfolds and candlelight. Capital O for the scene (“the Oath”); lower case for the promise itself (“everyone swore an oath”).

## Never

- Use the name, logo, music, catchphrases, presenter, set or format words of any TV programme. That includes “traitor”, “faithful”, “round table”, “banishment”, “shield”, “dagger”, “seer”, “recruit”, “ultimatum” and “mission” as game terms.
- Use Hasbro’s Betrayal vocabulary (“haunt”, “omen”, “heroes”, “the house on the hill”), and never write “Betrayal at…”.
- Name an in-game word in public material, or reveal the Confessor anywhere public.
- Call the product an Invitation or a Standing Invitation, or the opening the Sacrament.
- Sell the table size or the length of the night as a benefit.
- Show a face under the hood.
