# Words, names and naming

## The name
**Cloak & Candle**: both words capitalised, joined by an ampersand, no "The". Where an ampersand can't go (URLs, file names): "Cloak and Candle" or "cloakandcandle". Site: cloakandcandle.co.uk.

## Game words
Capitalised: **Cloak**, **Candle**, **Ghost**, **the Reckoning**, **the Champion**, **the Dilemma**, **the Witness** (internal only; publicly "a secret role" / "a special prize"), **Conversion**, **the Bargain**, **End or Exile**.
Lower case: shield, blade, pot, mission, mingle, night, dawn, exile, poison, murder, side-quest, party pass, room.

| word | means |
|---|---|
| Cloak | a secret killer; plural Cloaks. "The cloak" (lower case) is the object: under the cloak, pull the cloak away |
| Candle | an innocent guest; every guest has a candle on the TV |
| the Reckoning | the vote: everyone chalks a name, boards turn in seat order |
| exile / exiled | voted out at the Reckoning |
| poison / murder | the two ways the Cloaks kill (host's choice) |
| Ghost | a player who is out but stays in the game |
| mission | one of the party games |
| the pot | the shared winnings the missions build |
| shield / blade / the Champion | prizes: safe tonight / a vote that counts twice / both |
| End or Exile | the last secret vote: stop now, or exile again |
| share or take | the surviving Cloaks' final choice |
| party pass | the £15 code for one game; the subscription is £20 a month for unlimited games |
| room | a game's two screens: the TV at /g/CODE and the host at /g/CODE#host |

## Voice
Candlelit, dry, a little theatrical, never gory. Short sentences. The game speaks to the table like a host who knows more than they're saying: "Somewhere at your table sit the Cloaks." British spelling.

## Never
- The name, logo, music, catchphrases, presenter, set or format terms of any TV programme, including words like "traitor", "faithful", "round table", "banishment" or "murder in the castle" used as game terms. Cloak & Candle is independent and its own thing.
- Reveal the Witness in public material.
- Show a face under the cloak (see the brand guidelines).
