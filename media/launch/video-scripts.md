# Short videos: six scripts

These are for TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels, 15–35 seconds, vertical. Film real games wherever you can: the product already looks like a show, so the reactions are the ad. Get a clear yes from everyone whose face appears before you post, and say which clips might become paid ads.

Rules that carry over from the brand: never picture a face under the hood, never mention a TV programme, never explain the special prize, and no gore. A tap on the shoulder is a murder.

---

### 1 · The count (the best-performing format to test first)
- **0–2s:** the TV at the Reckoning, the tally at 4–4. Text: *"Last board. It's between me and my sister."*
- **2–10s:** the last chalkboard turns. Cut to the TV count ticking up.
- **10–15s:** the reveal card: LOYAL. Groans. Text: *"She was innocent. I was Forsworn."*
- **End card:** "The Betrayal · thebetrayal.co.uk"
- **Caption:** One of us was lying all night. It was me.

### 2 · Dawn
- **0–3s:** black screen, candles, the night music. Text: *"Everyone wakes up. One chair is empty."*
- **3–10s:** blindfolds off. The TV fades to dawn and the name appears in red.
- **10–15s:** that guest's face. Text: *"Their candle has gone out."*
- **Caption:** Dinner party rule one: never trust whoever poured the wine.

### 3 · Host POV: five minutes of setup
- A phone screen recording of the setup pages, scrolled at speed, with a voiceover:
  *"Twelve names. Three Forsworn. Poison, not murder. Dinner after round two. Start."*
- Cut to the TV title card lighting a candle for every guest.
- **Text:** *"I didn't learn a single rule. The phone told me what to say."*
- **Caption:** The easiest big party I've ever hosted.

### 4 · What you need (flat lay, no faces needed)
- Overhead shot. Place each item on the table on the beat: sleep masks, mini chalkboards, chalk, laptop, TV remote, a bottle of fizz with a label reading "the prize".
- **Text:** *"Everything you need for the best dinner party of your life:"* then list the items.
- **End:** *"Under £2 a guest."*
- **Caption:** 8–24 players. One £15 Invitation. No app, no accounts.

### 5 · The Sacrament
- A dim table, everyone blindfolded, candles on the TV. The host walks behind the chairs.
- The host's voice, low: *"If you feel my hand on your shoulder, you are Forsworn."*
- A hand lands on a shoulder. Freeze. The title card appears.
- **Caption:** Would you tell your friends?

### 6 · The awards
- The TV award cards appear one by one.
- **Text:** *"The Grand Deceiver goes to…"* Cut to the least likely person at the table (a grandparent, the quiet one).
- **Caption:** She lied to all of us all night. We're so proud.

---

**Posting:** about 3 a week on TikTok and Reels (the same video on both is fine). Hidden-role games are part of the national conversation on Thursday and Friday evenings through October, so post a strong one around 9:30pm on those nights.
