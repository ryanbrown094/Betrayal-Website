# Outreach templates

Replace anything in [brackets]. Keep it personal: these work because they come from you, not from a brand. Public words only (see `01-game/glossary-and-naming.md`): the twists are for the game to explain. Lead with Modern (a phone each, nothing to buy, the host plays too); mention Classic only if someone asks about blindfolds or playing without phones.

---

## 1 · WhatsApp to friends and family (week 1)

> I’ve built something and I’d love your help launching it. It’s called The Betrayal: a party game, best with 8 to 16 people, where a few guests are secretly killers and everyone else has one evening to find them. Everyone plays on their own phone, like a pub quiz: scan the code, type your name, and your secret role arrives on your screen. The host plays too, and nobody has to learn any rules.
>
> It’s live at thebetrayal.co.uk (£10 to book a Table for one party). If you’ve got a birthday, a Christmas do, a games night or a big dinner coming up, I’d love you to try it, and if you know anyone who hosts, please send them this. I’d really like honest feedback too.

## 2 · Asking a friend to be a seed host (a free Table)

> Would you host a game of The Betrayal for your friends in the next two weeks? I’ll give you a free Table. You need 8+ people with their phones, and about 3 to 3½ hours (a bit longer if you stop for dinner). A TV is a nice extra, not a must. You play too: the game runs itself, and your phone just says when to begin the next bit. In return, I’d like a quick review afterwards and, if everyone’s happy, a couple of short clips (the vote and the reveals are the best bits). No pressure if not.

## 6 · Reddit (read each subreddit’s self-promotion rules first; many only allow it in set threads)

> **I made a hidden-role party game that everyone plays on their own phone, host included**
>
> I built this for a party at my own house and it went down well, so I turned it into a proper product. Guests scan a QR code on the TV or go to a /join page and type a code and their name, pub-quiz style. The Oath is a secret question on each phone, roles arrive privately, all the Trials (quizzes, word games, rock-paper-scissors, charades where only the actor’s phone shows the word) are played on phones, and so is the vote. At night the Forsworn choose on their phones while everyone else taps too, so nobody can tell who’s choosing. The host plays as well: the game plans itself and the host’s phone just moves it on. The TV is optional. It’s best with 8 to 16 players and takes about 3 to 3½ hours for 8 to 12, a bit longer if you stop for dinner (it can serve dinner in courses between the rounds, but that’s off unless you want it). If you’d rather do it the old-fashioned way, there’s also a Classic mode with a Keeper, blindfolds and chalkboards.
>
> It’s £10 a party, or £20 a month if you host often. I’d love feedback from people who run big game nights. What would stop you trying it? [link]
