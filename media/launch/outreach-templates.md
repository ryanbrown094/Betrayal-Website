# Outreach templates

Replace anything in [brackets]. Keep it personal: these work because they come from you, not from a brand. Public words only (see `01-game/glossary-and-naming.md`): the twists are for the TV to explain.

---

## 1 · WhatsApp to friends and family (week 1)

> I’ve built something and I’d love your help launching it. It’s called The Betrayal: a dinner-party game, best with 8 to 16 people, where a few guests are secretly killers and everyone else has one evening to find them. The TV runs the show and the host’s phone runs the game, so nobody has to learn any rules.
>
> It’s live at thebetrayal.co.uk (£15 to book a Table for one party). If you’ve got a birthday, a Christmas do or a big dinner coming up, I’d love you to try it, and if you know anyone who hosts, please send them this. Honest feedback is gold.

## 2 · Asking a friend to be a seed host (a free Table)

> Would you host a game of The Betrayal for your friends in the next two weeks? I’ll give you a free Table. You need 8+ people, a TV and laptop, and about 3 to 4 hours with dinner (or about 2 hours for the Short game, if there are 8 to 10 of you). If you’d rather play than host, a friend can keep for you. In return: a quick review afterwards, and if everyone’s happy, a couple of short clips (the vote count and the reveals are the best bits). No pressure if not.
