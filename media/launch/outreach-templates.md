# Outreach templates

Replace anything in [brackets]. Keep it personal: these work because they come from you, not from a brand.

---

## 1 · WhatsApp to friends and family (week 1)

> I've built something and I'd love your help launching it. It's called The Betrayal: a dinner-party game for 8–24 people where a few guests are secretly killers and everyone else has one evening to find them. The TV runs the show and the host's phone runs the game, so nobody has to learn any rules.
>
> It's live at thebetrayal.co.uk (£15 an Invitation). If you've got a birthday, a Christmas do or a big dinner coming up, I'd love you to try it, and if you know anyone who hosts, please send them this. Honest feedback is gold.

## 2 · Asking a friend to be a seed host (free Invitation)

> Would you host a game of The Betrayal for your friends in the next two weeks? I'll give you a free Invitation. You need 8+ people, a TV and laptop, and about 3½ hours. In return: a quick review afterwards, and if everyone's happy, a couple of short clips (the vote count and the reveals are the best bits). No pressure if not.
