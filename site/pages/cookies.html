Cookie notice

Last updated: 29 September 2026

The Betrayal stores a few small things it needs to work. The law doesn’t require us to ask your consent for these, but we want you to know what they are. We also ask whether we may use Google’s advertising cookies, which tell us which of our adverts bring people here. They stay off unless you say yes, and the game itself (your Table and every player’s phone) never uses them.

Google Tag Manager is on the site’s pages either way, in Google’s Consent Mode. Until you say yes it sets and reads no cookies and sends Google only a basic signal that a page was viewed or a Table booked, with no identifier for you (your browser’s request carries its IP address, as any request does). Google uses those signals to estimate how many bookings its adverts led to.

Payments

When you pay, you’re taken to Stripe’s secure checkout page. Stripe sets its own cookies there, which it needs to take the payment and prevent fraud. See Stripe’s cookie policy.

Controlling them

You can change your advertising choice at any time with the button above. You can also clear or block cookies and browser storage in your browser’s settings. If you do, the Keeper will have to re-enter the PIN, and the preview password will be asked for again.

Questions? See our privacy notice or contact us.
