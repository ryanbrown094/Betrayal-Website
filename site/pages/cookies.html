Cookie notice

Last updated: 29 September 2026

The Betrayal uses no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. It only stores the small things it needs to work. The law doesn’t require us to ask your consent for these, but we want you to know what they are.

Payments

When you pay, you’re taken to Stripe’s secure checkout page. Stripe sets its own cookies there, which it needs to take the payment and prevent fraud. See Stripe’s cookie policy.

Controlling them

You can clear or block cookies and browser storage in your browser’s settings. If you do, the Keeper will have to re-enter the PIN, and the preview password will be asked for again.
