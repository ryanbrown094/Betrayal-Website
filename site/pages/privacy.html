Privacy notice

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This notice explains what personal information The Betrayal collects, why, and what your rights are. We collect as little as we can: there are no accounts, and advertising cookies are only used if you say yes.

Who is responsible for your data

The Betrayal is the controller of your personal data under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Contact us through our contact form or at info@thebetrayal.co.uk.

Guests at a party

If you’re a guest, your first name or a nickname is in the game: you typed it yourself when you joined (in Modern), or the host entered it (in Classic). It appears on the phones and the TV, we only use it to run that game, and it is deleted with the Table. We ask everyone to use first names or nicknames only. If you want your name removed sooner, ask the host, who can rename or remove you, or contact us.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell your data or share it for marketing. We use these providers, who process it for us under contract:

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: hosts the website and the game rooms, sends your booking code to you by email, and forwards contact messages and problem reports to our inbox.
  • Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. and its group: takes payments. Stripe also emails your receipt, and uses payment data for fraud prevention as an independent controller. See stripe.com/gb/privacy.
  • Google Ireland Limited (Google Ads): its tag runs on the site’s pages. If you say yes to advertising cookies, it tells Google Ads when a visitor who clicked one of our adverts books a Table, with the amount and a booking reference. If you don’t, it uses no cookies and sends only a basic signal that a page was viewed or a Table booked, which Google uses to estimate how many bookings its adverts led to. Google uses this data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy.

We may also share data where the law requires it.

International transfers

Cloudflare, Stripe and Google may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. When they do, the transfer is protected by the UK–US data bridge or by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your data, and to have it corrected or deleted. You can also object to or restrict how we use it, and ask for it in a portable format. Ask us through our contact form or at info@thebetrayal.co.uk. We’ll reply within 24 hours, and complete your request within one month. There is normally no charge.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We’d appreciate the chance to put it right first.

We only use what the site needs to work. See our cookie notice.

Changes

If we change this notice we’ll update the date at the top. If a change affects you significantly, we’ll tell you.
