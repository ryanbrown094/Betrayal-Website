Contact us

Questions about a booking, a problem on the night, or a refund: send us a message here, or email info@thebetrayal.co.uk, and we’ll reply within 1–2 working days. On a Friday or Saturday evening we aim to reply within an hour. If something goes wrong mid-game, send us your six-character Table code here and what happened. In Classic, “Report a problem” on the Keeper’s phone or the TV is quickest: it sends us a snapshot of the screen too.

Never send us your Keeper PIN.

Lost your Table?

Go to the home page and enter your booking code again: within its 48 hours, the same Table comes back.
