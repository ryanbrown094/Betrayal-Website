A night of The Betrayal

These pictures show Classic, where one phone runs the game; in Modern everyone plays on their own phone instead (see the end of this page). In Classic, two screens run the party. The TV is for your guests: the candles, the Trials, the votes and the reveals. The Keeper’s phone is private: it knows who the Forsworn are and tells the Keeper exactly what to say and when. These are real screenshots from a ten-player game, in the order you’d see them. Labels show which screen each one is from.

Before the guests arrive

The Keeper sets the game up on their phone: five questions (who’s playing, how long, murder or poison, the prize, who keeps), and the rest is planned from the answers. Every choice is explained, and any of it can be changed before the game starts. A first game keeps the twists off, so everyone learns the game itself.
