A night at Cloak & Candle

Two screens run the party. The TV is for your guests: the candles, the missions, the votes and the reveals. The host’s phone is private: it knows who the Cloaks are and tells the host exactly what to say and when. These are real screenshots from a ten-player game, in the order you’d see them. Labels show which screen each one is from.

Before the guests arrive

The host sets the game up on their phone. It’s one question a page, and every page explains what the choice does to the evening. Each one has a recommended answer, worked out from the choices before it, so a first-time host can simply tap Next.
